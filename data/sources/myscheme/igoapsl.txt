Indira Gandhi Old Age Pension Scheme (Ladakh)
Indira GandhiOld AgePensionSenior Citizen
Details
The Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme, under the National Social Assistance Programme, has been implemented by the Social and Tribal Welfare Department, Government of Ladakh, to provide financial assistance to old-age persons aged 60 years and above living below the poverty line (BPL), and the scheme is centrally sponsored and jointly funded by the Central and State Governments.
 

Benefits
Under this scheme, a financial benefit of ₹1,000/- is provided to BPL beneficiaries aged 60 years and above.
 

Eligibility
The applicant should be a resident of Ladakh.
The applicant should be a senior citizen.
The applicant should be 60 years of age or older.
The applicant should belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.
The applicant should not be receiving pension benefits under any social security scheme.
 

Application Process
Offline
Step 1: Obtain the application form free of cost from the DSWO/TSWO office, fill it in completely, and attach the required documents related to age, income, status, and address.
 

Step 2: Submit the completed application to the concerned Tehsil Social Welfare Officer (TSWO) and obtain a proper receipt.
 

Step 3: The TSWO consolidates eligible applications and forwards them to the District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO) for fund arrangement and sanction by the District Level Sanctioning Committee.
 

Documents Required
Aadhaar card of the applicant
Passport-size photograph
Identity proof i.e. Voter ID Card, Driving License
Residence Proof
Proof of age
Below Poverty Line (BPL) Card/related documents
Caste certificate, if applicable
Address proof
Bank Passbook/Bank account details
Any other documents as required
