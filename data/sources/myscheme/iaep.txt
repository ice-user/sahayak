Ministry Of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
Innovation & Agri-Entrepreneurship Program (RKVY)
AgriculturalDPIITFarmingStartups
Details
The Innovation & Agri-Entrepreneurship Program (RKVY), under the Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India, supports agri-startups through funding, mentorship, and incubation to develop innovative solutions for agriculture and contribute to strengthening the agricultural sector.
OBJECTIVES
• To promote innovation and agri-preneurship by providing financial support and nurturing the incubation ecosystem.
• To strengthen infrastructure in agriculture and allied areas.
 

Benefits
• Pre-seed support up to ₹5 lakh and seed-stage support up to ₹25 lakh via R-ABIs.
• Provides grant-in-aid, incubation and mentoring support to eligible agri startups.
 

 

Eligibility
DPIIT-recognised startups working in the agriculture and allied sectors are eligible to apply.
 

Application Process
Online
 

Step 1: Visit the official Agri-Startup portal: Agri-Startup Portal 
Step 2: On the login page, enter your registered User ID/Email ID and Password.
Step 3: If you are a new user, select the Registration/New User option and provide the required details.
Step 4: Enter the details of your startup/organisation, including the applicable registration and contact information.
Step 5: Complete the required verification and submit the registration form.
Step 6: After successful registration, use your registered credentials to log in to the portal.
Step 7: After login, look for the relevant RKVY / Innovation & Agri-Entrepreneurship Program application or call.
Step 8: Fill in the required startup, innovation, project, and funding details.
Step 9: Upload the required supporting documents and submit the application.
Step 10: Save the application/reference number for future tracking and communication.
 

Documents Required
DPIIT Recognition Certificate
Certificate of Incorporation/Registration
Detailed Business Plan/Project Proposal
Pitch Deck/Startup Profile
Financial and Bank Account Details
