Details
The scheme "Pradhan Mantri Matasya Sampada Yojana: Cycle with Ice Boxes" by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying, Government of India, aims to provide central assistance for the cost of cycles with ice boxes to beneficiaries involved in fisheries activities. Through this scheme, central assistance for Cycle with Ice Box costs is provided for the initial crop only in the purchase of newly purchased cycles with ice boxes.
 

Benefits
The scheme provides a subsidy for the cycle with ice boxes for fisheries activities. 
The project cost is ₹0.10/- lakh per unit.
The subsidy will be limited to 40% of the project cost for beneficiaries falling under the General Category.
The subsidy will be limited to 60% of the project cost for beneficiaries who are Scheduled Caste (SC) or Women.
 

Note: The Government of India and the Government of Haryana shall not be responsible for any losses incurred on procurement, operation, maintenance, and management of the fish transport facilities.
 

Eligibility
Beneficiary must have Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP).
Beneficiaries should ensure that fish transport facilities are maintained in operational condition.
Beneficiaries shall be provided central assistance for Cycle with Ice box the initial crop only in the purchase of newly purchased Cycle with Ice Box
Central assistance for Cycle with Ice Box cost shall be released only for the fisheries activity.
Govt. of India and Govt. of Haryana shall not be responsible for any losses incurred on procurement, operation, maintenance and management of the fish transport facilities.
 

 

Application Process
Online
Registration Process on Antyodaya-SARAL Portal:
Step 1: Visit the Official Portal: Antyodaya-SARAL Portal. 
Step 2: For registration, click on “New User/Register Here” and fill in all the mandatory details, i.e. Name, Email ID, Mobile Number & password. Click on ‘Submit’.
Step 3: Verify the OTP received in the provided Email and Mobile Number. Alternatively, click on the Verification Link to activate the profile.
 

Login to Apply for the Scheme:
Step 1: Visit the Official Portal: Antyodaya-SARAL Portal. 
Step 2: The “Sign in here” option is available on the right side of the screen. Fill in the required credentials and click on the ‘Login’ button.
Step 3: In the opened window, click on “Scheme/Services list”, and a list of schemes will appear on the screen.
Step 4: Now, select the scheme and click on “Apply for Service/Scheme”.
Step 5: Fill in all the details in the online application form and click on "Submit".

Documents Required
Agreement Deed between Vendor and Fisheries Department.
Date of Birth Certificate (Voting Card/Birth Certificate/Aadhaar Card/Matriculation Certificate).
Caste Certificate from First Class Magistrate (Tehsildar).
Training Certificate of Vendor/Beneficiary.
Bills/Receipts/Vouchers.
Photographs of Beneficiary with Cycle with Ice-Box.
Detail of Bank Account and PAN Card.
Detailed Project Report (DPR)/Self Contained Proposal (SCP) as per Pradhan Mantri Matasya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) guidelines.
