Goa
Bal Yuwa Kirtankar Festival
AcademyBoys GirlsChildrenFestival
Details
The Bal-Yuwa Kirtankar Festival is a biannual event organized by the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Goa, aimed at encouraging children and young performers up to 16 years old to promote the traditional art of Kirtan. Selection of participants is conducted by Kala Academy and Haribhakt Parishad, Goa. Selected performers receive cash remuneration for their performances along with reimbursement of actual transport fares.
Benefits
Bal Category:
First Prize ₹ 5,000/-
Second Prize ₹ 3,000/-
Third Prize ₹ 2,000/-
3 Consolation prizes of ₹ 1,000/- each
Yuva Category:
First Prize ₹ 7,000/-
Second Prize ₹ 5,000/-
Third Prize ₹ 3,000/-
3 Consolation prizes of ₹ 2,000/- each
Assistance available : ₹ 500/- per performance/transport fare.
 

Eligibility
The Bal applicant: Boys and girls aged 7 to 14 years are eligible to participate.
The Yuva applicant: Boys and girls aged 15 to 21 years are eligible to participate.
 

Application Process
Offline
Step 1: Visit Kala Academy Goa and obtain the application form free of charge.
Step 2: Complete the form and submit it to the Member Secretary, Kala Academy Goa within the specified deadline.
Step 3: For any issues or complaints, contact the Member Secretary or Chairman of Kala Academy Goa.
Documents Required
ID Proof (e.g., Aadhaar card, PAN card, etc.)
Address Proof (e.g., Aadhaar card, electricity bill, etc.)
