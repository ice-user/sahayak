DAE-Homi Sethna Chair (DAE-HSC)
Atomic EnergyNuclear PowerResearch And DevelopmentRetired EngineersRetired Scientists
Details
The scheme “DAE-Homi Sethna Chair (DAE-HSC)” implemented by the Department of Atomic Energy aims to utilize the expertise of retired scientists, engineers, and technologists through providing opportunities to undertake research and development, policy studies, and planning activities in critical and strategic sectors related to atomic energy. Under the scheme, selected awardees work on projects and policy issues identified by the Department of Atomic Energy. The awardees may also participate in public outreach activities, academic lectures, mentorship of young scientists and engineers, and preparation of technical publications or books related to atomic energy. The scheme provides a monthly honorarium and support grant to eligible awardees during the approved tenure. Nominations are invited through host institutes throughout the year and are reviewed by a high-level committee constituted by the Chairman, Atomic Energy Commission.
 

Benefits
Monthly Honorarium:
The awardee receives an honorarium of ₹1,00,000/- per month.
The total of honorarium and pension must not exceed the last pay drawn by the awardee at the time of retirement.
Support Grant:
The awardee receives a support grant of ₹1,50,000/- per annum.
The support grant can be used for local commuting to the host institute and domestic travel for official work.
Research Opportunity:
The awardee gets an opportunity to work on critical and strategic research, policy, and planning projects identified by the Department of Atomic Energy.
Academic And Mentorship Activities:
The awardee may participate in lectures, mentoring young scientists and engineers, and public outreach programmes.
Leave Facility:
The awardee is entitled to 30 days leave per year with honorarium.
 

Eligibility
The applicant must be a retired scientist, engineer, or technologist from units of the Department of Atomic Energy, including aided institutions.
Preference is given to applicants involved in front-line scientific research, development of sensitive and/or critical technologies in DAE units, and fellows of major science or engineering academies.
The applicant must have rich experience in the selected research area.
The applicant must possess domain knowledge related to the selected topic.
The applicant must have expertise in the area of the topic selected for the award.
 

Application Process
Online
Step 1: The applications are on a nomination basis. The head of the host institute interested in hosting the candidates shall send the nomination as a PDF attachment to the email id: hscadmin@dae.gov.in
