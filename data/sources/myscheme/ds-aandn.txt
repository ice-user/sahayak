Details
The “Dulari Scheme” was launched by the Health Department, Union Territory of Andaman and Nicobar Islands on 26th January, 2013 to give incentives to newborn baby girls and girl children on passing VIII, X & XII standards. As per the present scheme, the girl child born on or after 26.01.2013 will be eligible to get the benefit under the scheme for birth incentives and the girl children who are below 13 years of age not registered under the Planned family scheme (old scheme) will also get the benefit of this scheme.
Benefits
As per the scheme, the following benefits are given to the girl child whose parents are domicile of Andaman & Nicobar Islands:
 

Sl. No. Descriptions Amount of Assistance
1 Benefits at Birth- Institution deliver ₹8,000/-
2 Benefits at Birth- Home delivery ₹5,000/-
3 Benefits on passing VIII Standard ₹5,000/-
4 Benefits on passing X Standard ₹10,000/-
5 Benefits on passing XII Standard ₹10,000/-
6 Benefits on getting married at the age of 21 years ₹5,000/-
7 Benefits on getting married after age of 25 years ₹10,000/-
 

Note- The incentive amount will be fixed in a bank account and must not be withdrawn before the girl attains 18 years of age, unless permitted by the A&N Administration.
 

Eligibility
The applicant must be a domicile of the Union Territory of Andaman & Nicobar Islands.
The applicant must be a woman or a girl child to avail benefits under the scheme.
The applicant must be born on or after 01.10.2013 to receive full benefits.
Girl children between 0–13 years, born before 01.10.2013 and not covered under the Planned Family Scheme, should be eligible only for educational incentives after passing Class VIII.
Exclusions
Benefits will cease if:
The applicant or her parents provide false or concealed information.
The girl gets married before attaining 21 years of age.
The girl drops out of school (as further education-based incentives will not be granted).
In case of the girl’s death before reaching the eligible disbursal age, the Directorate of Health Services may permit premature closure of the deposit, and the applicable amount may be released to the parents.
Application Process
Online
Registration on Portal:
Step 01: Visit official Portal 
. 
Step 02: On the home page, click on ‘Register’ and fill in all the mandatory details.
Step 03: Click on the ‘Submit’ button and validate your Email ID & Mobile Number using OTP received.
Step 04: After successful validation, the applicant will be registered.
 

Registration for Dulari:
Step 01: Applicant can login on official portal through their email ID that is used while registering
Step 02: Enter Password & Captcha
Step 03: Click on ‘Login’
Step 04: After successful login, click on ‘Apply for Services’ and then ‘View all Available Services’
Step 05: Now, search for ‘Dulari’ and click on ‘Dulari Registration’.
Step 06: Fill in all the mandatory details, preview the form, and click on ‘Submit’.
Step 07: After successful registration, the applicant will receive a registration ID. 
Documents Required
Identity proof of parent (s) i.e. Aadhaar Card
Passport-size photograph of parent (s)
Birth Certificate of child
Local certificate/domicile certificate/permanent resident certificate 
Ration Card of Family
Address proof of the parent(s)
Mark sheet of Class XIII/X/XII, if applicable
Bonafide certificate from the school or school leaving certificate, if applicable
Marriage registration certificate, if applicable
Bank account details/Copy of passbook
Any other document as required
