Innovative Agro Forestry for Food & Nutrition Security: Trees on Farm Land
AssistanceCapacity BuildingSoil & Water ConservationTree Plantation
Details
The scheme “Innovative Agroforestry for Food & Nutrition Security: Trees on farm Land” has been launched by the Agriculture & Farmers’ Empowerment Department, Government of Odisha. It aims to promote system-based agroforestry in an integrated manner and strengthen the economic and social well-being of farmers. Through this scheme, assistance for the plantation of fruit, timber, and multipurpose trees on farm lands, along with soil and water conservation measures, is provided to eligible farmers. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Soil Conservation & Watersheds through district and block-level functionaries.
Objectives
To encourage and expand tree plantation in a complementary and integrated manner with crops and livestock.
To ensure the availability of quality planting material.
To popularise agroforestry practices/models suitable for different agro-ecological regions and land use conditions.
To provide extension and capacity building support to the agroforestry sector.
 

Benefits
Financial assistance is provided for the plantation of trees on farm lands under defined agroforestry models.
Assistance for Trees on Farm Lands – Model I is provided at ₹3,00,000/- per hectare over 3 years in a 50:25:25 proportion.
Assistance for Trees on Farm Lands – Model II is provided at ₹2,00,000/- per hectare over 3 years in a 50:25:25 proportion.
Assistance for Trees on Farm Lands – Model III is provided at ₹1,00,000/- per hectare over 3 years in a 50:25:25 proportion.
Assistance for Trees on Farm Lands – Model IV is provided at ₹300/- per tree over 3 years in a 50:25:25 proportion.
Assistance for Demonstration of Innovative Agroforestry Model (1 hectare) is provided at ₹5,00,000 per hectare over 3 years in a 60:20:20 proportion.
 

Eligibility
The applicant must be a resident/domicile of the state of Odisha.
The applicant must have a valid Farmer ID in the Krushak Odisha portal of the Government of Odisha.
The applicant must possess farm land suitable for the proposed agroforestry model, preferably with a protective irrigation source.
The applicant must be willing to adopt agroforestry interventions in the field of agroforestry and take up proposed interventions in a system mode - plantation of horticulture / Multipurpose trees, in situ soil conservation measures, and intercrops in an integrated manner.
The preference will be given to applicants fromthe Scheduled Caste, the Scheduled Tribe, small farmers, marginal farmers, or Forest Rights Act beneficiary and other vulnerable groups.
 

Application Process
Offline
Step 1: The applicant farmer submits an application for assistance under “Trees on Farm Land” in the prescribed Application Format (Annexure II) appended to the guidelines.
Step 2: In the application form, the farmer furnishes personal details, Farmer ID, Aadhaar number, bank account details, and complete land particulars, including Mouza, Khata number, Plot number, area, Forest Rights Act status, and current land use, as specified in Annexure II.
Step 3: The farmer indicates the proposed agroforestry model, species-wise tree requirement, soil and water conservation measures, and proposed allied activities in the relevant section of the application form as per Annexure II.
Step 4: The application is submitted to the concerned field functionary at the village or block level for preliminary scrutiny.
Step 5: The field functionaries verify the eligibility of the applicant, land suitability, and feasibility of the proposed agroforestry intervention through field inspection.
Step 6: Based on verification, the proposal is examined and consolidated at the block level, followed by scrutiny and approval at the district level as per roles defined in the scheme implementation framework.
Step 7: Approved proposals are incorporated into the Annual Action Plan prepared by the district and submitted to the competent authority for final approval.
Step 8: After approval, assistance is released for the approved agroforestry intervention as per the model-wise cost norms and phased pattern of assistance specified in Annexure IV.
Step 9: Monitoring, verification, and reporting of implementation are carried out periodically by designated officials as per the Monitoring and Evaluation mechanism laid down in the guidelines.
