Chief Minister’s Meritorious student fellowship
EducationFellowshipMeritoriousStudent
Details
The Chief Minister’s Meritorious Student Fellowship (CMMSF) is a scheme under the Department of School Education, Government of Nagaland. This is the state sponsored scheme given to the meritorious who have secured Top 50 positions in the High School leaving certificate examination conducted by the Nagaland board of School Education. Selection of student and disbursement of scholarship amount is implemented by the Nagaland Board of School Education. 
 

 

Benefits
The amount disbursed is ₹50,000/- per year for each student for a period of 5 Years, provide that the individual student score not less than 80% marks in the promotional / annual examination in the succeeding classes every year.
 

Eligibility
The applicant must belong to an Indigenous Tribe of Nagaland.
The applicant must have secured a position among the Top 50 rank holders in the High School Leaving Certificate (HSLC) Examination conducted by the Nagaland Board of School Education (NBSE).
The applicant must have successfully qualified the HSLC Examination conducted by NBSE.
The selected student must secure a minimum of 80% marks in the promotional/annual examination in each succeeding academic year to continue receiving the fellowship.
 

 

Application Process
Offline
To apply for the scheme, candidates had to download 
1.Form for Chief Minister s Meritorious Students Fellowship.
2. Endorsement Certificate for CMMSF from the Principal or Head of the Institute.
3. Bond Certificate for Chief Minister s Meritorious Students Fellowship.
From the official NBSE website (https://nbsenl.edu.in/scholarships) and submit through(Email to cmmsf50ng@gmail.com)
 

 

 

Documents Required
Duly filled CMMSF Application Form.
Copy of HSLC Mark Sheet issued by NBSE.
Indigenous Tribe Certificate of Nagaland.
Endorsement Certificate signed by the Principal/Head of the Institution.
Duly completed Bond Certificate for CMMSF.
Any other supporting documents required by NBSE.
