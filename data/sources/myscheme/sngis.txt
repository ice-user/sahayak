Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology
STPI – Next Generation Incubation Scheme (NGIS)
CompanyDPIITInternshipStartupsTechnology
Details
Next Generation Incubation Scheme (NGIS), under the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India, is a government scheme that helps nurture early-stage tech startups by providing incubation support, funding, and mentorship so they can develop strong, scalable business ideas.
OBJECTIVES
• It is a startup incubation scheme by Software Technology Parks of India (STPI) under MeitY, aimed at strengthening India’s software product startup ecosystem, especially in Tier-II/III cities. It aims to handhold 300 tech startups across 12 STPI incubation locations in Tier-II/III cities.
 

Benefits
• Seed / risk funding up to ₹25 lakh for promising startups.
• Access to state-of-the-art incubation infrastructure, labs, equipment, and testing facilities.
• Mentoring, startup-connect, industry networking, marketing support, and product lifecycle guidance.
• Internship / challenge grants for selected startups
 

Eligibility
• Indian startups working on software product development / technology-based solutions.
• DPIIT-recognised startups are encouraged; individuals, academicians, researchers, entrepreneurs, LLPs, and partnership firms may also participate but may need to register as a private limited company if selected.
 

Application Process
Online
Applicants can submit their proposals online through the STPI Startup Portal during open NGIS application calls.
 

Documents Required
Certificate of Incorporation/Startup Registration
DPIIT Recognition Certificate (if applicable)
Detailed Business/Project Proposal
Pitch Deck or Startup Profile
Company/Founder Profile and Financial Details
