Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom: Organic Farming: Construction of vermicompost unit (nos)
AgricultureFarmFarmingSpices
Details
The Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom – Organic Farming: Construction of Vermicompost Units is implemented by the Spices Board, India, under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India. The scheme aims to encourage spice growers to produce vermicompost on their farms by establishing vermicompost units. This helps in the production of high-quality organic manure, improves soil fertility, and promotes the organic cultivation of spices. The scheme is being implemented in accordance with the modalities and guidelines approved by the Competent Authority under the Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26).
 

Benefits
33.33% of cost one-ton capacity of vermicompost unit @ ₹ 4500/- for Gen and 75% @ ₹ 10,000/- for SC/ST
Spices growers having holdings from 0.10 ha to 8.00 ha. A grower having an area upto 4 ha will be eligible to avail a maximum of 1 unit and a grower having area above 4 ha and upto 8 ha will be eligible to avail a maximum of 2 units under the scheme.
The size of the vermicompost unit construction will be 2.7 m x 0.9 m x 0.9 m with a capacity to produce one ton in two cycles.
Locally available customized units with the above production capacity are also eligible for assistance with supporting bills and documents of purchase and the assistance will be limited to eligible % of assistance of actual cost.
 

 

Eligibility
1. All the spice growing States and regions/UTs of India
2. Designated Officials will conduct final inspection and recommend the eligible cases.
3. Preference will be given to farmers who are members of ICS and groups/FPOs
 

Application Process
Online
Registration
Step 1: The applicant should visit the ServicesPlus website.
Step 2: Click on the “Register” option on the homepage.
Step 3: Fill out the registration form by entering Full Name, Email ID, Mobile Number, Password, selecting State, and Captcha.
Step 4: Review all details carefully and submit the registration form.
Step 5: An OTP will be sent to your registered mobile number or email; enter the OTP and click “Verify.”
Step 6: Registration will be completed successfully, and a confirmation message will be displayed or sent via email.
Login
Step 1: The applicant should visit the ServicesPlus website and click on “Login.” 
Step 2: Enter your Email ID, Password, and Captcha.
Step 3: Select the relevant service/scheme name.
Step 4: Fill in the required details in the application form.
Step 5: Submit the application.
 

Documents Required
a) Aadhaar Card
b) Current year land tax receipt / parcha
c) Bank Pass book first page
