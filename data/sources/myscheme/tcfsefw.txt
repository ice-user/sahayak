Meghalaya
Training Centre for Self Employment for Women
DestituteEmploymentTrainingWomen
Details
The scheme Training Centre for Self-Employment for Women, under the Social Welfare Department, Government of Meghalaya, provides training to destitute women in various trades such as tailoring, knitting, embroidery, and weaving to help them become self-employed. The scheme also provides a stipend to the beneficiaries during the training.
 

Benefits
Under this scheme, training is provided to destitute women in various trades such as tailoring, knitting, embroidery, weaving , and stipend.
 

Eligibility
The person must be a resident of Meghalaya.
The applicant should be a destitute woman.
 

Application Process
Offline
Applications through the office of the Superintendent Training Centre for women in need of care and protection.
 

Documents Required
Aadhaar Card or valid identity proof.
Age proof.
Address/residence proof.
Passport-size photograph.
Any other document required
