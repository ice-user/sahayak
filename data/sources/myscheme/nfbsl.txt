Ladakh
National Family Benefit Scheme (Ladakh)
BPLDeath Of BreadwinnerFamilyFinancial Assistance
Details
The "National Family Benefit Scheme", implemented under the National Social Assistance Programme by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Ladakh, is a centrally sponsored scheme jointly funded by the Central and State Governments, which provides a one-time lump sum assistance of ₹20,000/- to Below Poverty Line (BPL) families on the death of the primary bread-earner aged 18–59 years, with the objective of ensuring social security, and the assistance is paid to the widow or unmarried daughter of the deceased.
 

Benefits
Provides a one-time lump sum cash assistance of ₹20,000/- to Below Poverty Line families on the death of the primary bread-earner.
 

Eligibility
The applicant should be a native of Ladakh.
The applicant should be a family living below the poverty line (BPL).
The applicant should have lost the primary breadwinner of the family.
The applicant should be the widow or unmarried daughter of the deceased.
The deceased bread-earner should have been between 18 and 59 years of age at the time of death.
 

Application Process
Offline
Step1: The applicant collects and fills the form from DSWO/TSWO with the required documents.
Step2: The completed form is submitted to the TSWO, who forwards it to the Block Level Sanctioning Committee.
Step3: The DSWO gets approval from the District Level Sanctioning Committee, and the payment is released to the beneficiary.
 

Documents Required
Aadhaar card of the applicant
Passport-size photograph
Identity proof 
Death certificate
Proof of age 
Income certificate
Below Poverty Line (BPL) Card/related documents
Address proof
Bank Passbook/Post Office Account Passbook
Any other documents as required
