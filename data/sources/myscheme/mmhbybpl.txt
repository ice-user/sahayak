Rajasthan
Mukhya Mantri Hamari Betiyan Yojana-BPL
BPLEducationFinancial AssistanceGirl
Details
The scheme “Mukhya Mantri Hamari Betiyan Yojana-BPL” launched by the Education (Elementary and Secondary) Department, Government of Rajasthan, aims to promote higher education among meritorious girl students belonging to the BPL category by providing financial assistance and recognition to those securing top positions in the Class 10 board examination.
 

Benefits
Financial Assistance: Eligible girl students are provided monetary support under the scheme.
 

Eligibility
The applicant must be a native of Rajasthan.
The applicant must belong to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.
Only one girl from the BPL category in each district is eligible for the scheme.
The applicant must have secured either first or second place in the Class 10 board examination conducted by the Ajmer Board of Secondary Education, Rajasthan.
The applicant must be studying in Class 11.
The applicant must have obtained a minimum of 75% marks in Class 10.
 

Application Process
Offline
Please contact your school.
 

Documents Required
1. Aadhaar Card.
2. Marksheet of class 10th.
3. Bank Account Statement.
4. Passport size photo.
5. Bhamashah Card.
6. Caste certificate.
