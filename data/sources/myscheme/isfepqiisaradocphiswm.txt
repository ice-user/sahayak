Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom: Post-Harvest Improvement: Spice Washing Machines (nos )
AgricultureFarmerFarmingSpice Washing MachinesSpices Board
Details
Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom: Post-Harvest Improvement – Spice Washing Machines (Nos.) is implemented by the Spices Board, India under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
The objective of the scheme is to promote the adoption of Spice Washing Machines to increase profitability in spice production through mechanization and improve the quality of spices for export.
The scheme is being implemented in accordance with the modalities and guidelines approved by the Competent Authority under the Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26).
 

Benefits
50 % of cost of unit, subject to a maximum of ₹ 53,000 /- per unit for General Category and 75% subject to maximum of ₹ 80,000/- per unit for NE/SC/ST & 90% Spice Producer's Groups in Identified Clusters subject to maximum of ₹ 95,000/-.
Spices growers having a land holding size of 0.40 ha to 8 ha Assistance for groups/FPOs will be as in QGBG
 

 

Eligibility
1.All spice growing States and regions in India/UT.
2. Designated Officials will conduct inspection and recommend the eligible cases.
 

Application Process
Online
Registration
Step 1: The applicant should visit the ServicesPlus website.
Step 2: Click on the “Register” option on the homepage.
Step 3: Fill out the registration form by entering Full Name, Email ID, Mobile Number, Password, selecting State, and Captcha.
Step 4: Review all details carefully and submit the registration form.
Step 5: An OTP will be sent to your registered mobile number or email; enter the OTP and click “Verify.”
Step 6: Registration will be completed successfully, and a confirmation message will be displayed or sent via email.
Login
Step 1: The applicant should visit the ServicesPlus website and click on “Login.” 
Step 2: Enter your Email ID, Password, and Captcha.
Step 3: Select the relevant service/scheme name.
Step 4: Fill in the required details in the application form.
Step 5: Submit the application.
 

Documents Required
a) Aadhaar Card
b) Current year land tax receipt / chitta adangal/Parcha copy / Possession certificate indicating the total area /NoC for NE
c) Bank Pass book
d) CR /ACR if applicable e) Invoice and payment documents towards purchase from valid manufacturer of the equipment/device empaneled by Spices Board
