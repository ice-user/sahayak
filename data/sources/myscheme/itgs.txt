Madhya Pradesh
International Travel Grant Scheme
EventFinancial AssistanceInternational TravelResearchersScientists
Details
The “International Travel Grant Scheme” has been launched by the Science and Technology (Information Technology) Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme aims to provide financial support to scientists and researchers for international participation in science and technology related events. The scheme is implemented by the M.P. Council of Science & Technology (MPCST), Bhopal.
 

Benefits
Nature of Benefits
Financial assistance for international air travel (economy class).
Partial reimbursement of registration fees for international events.
Coverage includes domestic travel connected with international journey.
Monetary Benefits
Maximum assistance up to ₹40,000 per applicant.
Assistance is limited to 50% of travel fare and/or 50% of registration fee, subject to an overall ceiling of ₹40,000.
Conditions Associated With Benefits
Only one author per paper is eligible, preferably the first author.
Maximum 3 candidates may be supported for a single international event.
Assistance is reimbursed only after return to India.
Mode of Disbursement
Reimbursement through electronic bank transfer to the applicant’s bank account.
Frequency of Disbursement
One-time assistance per approved international event.
Assistance is valid only for the approved event and sanctioned travel period.
 

Eligibility
The applicant should be an employee of a recognized institution located in Madhya Pradesh.
The applicant should be a research scholar with at least 2 years of research experience.
The applicant should be affiliated with an institution recognized by a state or central authority.
The applicant should have received an official invitation or acceptance for participation.
The applicant should apply through the Head of the Institution.
The applicant should present research relevant to the development of Madhya Pradesh.
The applicant should not have availed MPCST International Travel Grant during the last 3 years.
 

 

Application Process
Offline
Step 1: The interested applicant downloads the application form from the official website. 
Step 2: In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
Step 3: A duly filled and signed application form along with the documents addressed to:
The Director General
M.P. Council of Science and Technology
Vigyan Bhawan, Nehru Nagar
Bhopal - 462003 (M.P.)
NOTE:
The applicant should apply through the Head of the Institution.
The applicant should submit the application at least 2 months before travel.
Incomplete applications are not eligible for consideration.
Post-visit documents are submitted within 1 month of return.
 

Documents Required
At the Application Stage
Invitation Or Acceptance Letter From Organizers
Full-Length Research Paper (4 Copies)
Bio-Data With List Of Publications (4 Copies)
Air India Fare Certificate (Economy Class)
Detailed Event Announcement And Technical Programme
At Reimbursement Stage (After Return)
Original Economy Class Air Ticket
Original Boarding Passes
Original Rail Or Bus Tickets (If Applicable)
Registration Fee Receipt With Currency Conversion Rate
Certificate Of Participation
Brief Participation Report
Proof Of Financial Support From Other Sources (If Any)
Cancelled Cheque With Account Name, Bank Name, Branch Name, Account Number, And Indian Financial System Code
