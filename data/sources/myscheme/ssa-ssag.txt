Ministry of Ports,Shipping and Waterways
Sagar Samman Award- Sagar Samman Award for Gallantry
AwardBraveryRiskSeafarer
Details
The "Sagar Samman Award- Sagar Samman Award for Gallantry" scheme has been launched by the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways under the Directorate General of Shipping, Government of India, aims to recognize extraordinary bravery in maritime situations. Under this scheme, recognition is provided to Indian seafarers who risk their lives to save others or protect the marine environment.
 

Benefits
Trophy
Citation
Individual Medals (if multiple persons)
 

Eligibility
The applicant should be an Indian seafarer serving on any vessel.
The applicant should have performed an act of exceptional bravery in adverse maritime conditions.
The applicant’s act should involve saving lives, property, or environment beyond normal duty.
 

Application Process
Online
Step 01: Visit the National Awards Portal: https://www.awards.gov.in 
Step 02: Register/Login using the official credentials of the recommending authority
Step 03: Fill in the nomination form with complete details
Step 04: Upload required documents, including citation and verification certificate
 

Documents Required
Incident Report
Eye Witness Statements
Identity Proof
Rank Details
Legal Heir Details (If Posthumous)
