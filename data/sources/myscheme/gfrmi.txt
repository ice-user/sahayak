Grant for Rare Musical Instruments
Cultural HeritageFinancial AssistanceInstrumentsTraditional Music
Details
The "Grant for Rare Musical Instruments" scheme has been introduced by the Culture and Archaeology Department, Government of Chhattisgarh. The scheme provides financial assistance to eligible folk art groups for purchasing rare and traditional musical instruments, helping preserve Chhattisgarh's folk music heritage and cultural traditions
Objective:
To preserve and promote rare musical traditions and traditional musical instruments of Chhattisgarh by providing financial assistance to active folk art groups for purchasing musical instruments.
 

Benefits
Financial assistance/grant for the purchase of musical instruments.
Supports preservation of rare musical styles and traditional instruments.
Strengthens cultural activities and performances of folk art groups.
 

Eligibility
The applicant must be a registered/active folk art group in Chhattisgarh.
The group should be engaged in preserving and promoting traditional folk music and cultural heritage.
The application must be submitted in the prescribed format with required documents.
 

Application Process
Online
Application Process
Step 1: Portal Access and Login
The applicant (or the authorized operator) must visit the Sewa Setu portal (https://sewasetu.cgstate.gov.in/home?lang=en), and login using either their Citizen credentials or through the Lok Sewa Kendra (LSK) login system to proceed with the application.
Step 2: Application Submission
The user must navigate to the application form of the scheme that they wish to apply for. In the form, enter all the required details accurately. In the form, fill in all the mandatory fields and upload all the mandatory documents in the specified format and size. Ensure that all documents are clear, legible, and in the accepted file format before uploading.
Once all required documents are successfully uploaded and the entered information is verified by the applicant, they must finally submit the application.
Step 3: Application Reference Number Generation
The portal generates an Application Reference Number (ARN). The applicant should save the ARN for all future correspondence and status tracking regarding their submitted application.
Post-Application Process
Step 1: Verification by Anudan Section
The application is routed online to the Anudan Section, where the application details and supporting documents are examined for completeness, correctness, and compliance with the prescribed requirements.
Step 2: Decision by Anudan Section
Based on the verification:
If the application is found satisfactory, it is forwarded online to the Deputy Director for further examination.
If any discrepancy or deficiency is identified, the application is returned to the applicant for correction and resubmission.
Step 3: Examination by Deputy Director
The Deputy Director reviews the application, supporting documents, and other relevant records to assess eligibility and compliance with the prescribed guidelines.
Step 4: Decision by Deputy Director
Based on the examination:
If approved, the application is forwarded to the Director for final review and approval.
If additional information or documents are required, the application is returned to the Citizen/Operator for correction.
If the application is found ineligible, it is rejected with recorded remarks.
Step 5: Review by Director
The Director scrutinizes the application and supporting documents to ensure that all eligibility conditions and requirements have been fulfilled.
Step 6: Final Decision by Director
Based on the review:
If approved, the application is forwarded to the Anudan Section for further processing.
If additional information or documents are required, the application is returned to the Citizen/Operator for correction.
If the application does not meet the prescribed criteria, it is rejected with recorded remarks.
Step 7: Generation of Digitally Signed Sanction Order
Upon approval, the system generates a Digitally Signed Sanction Order using the authorized Digital Signature Certificate (DSC).
Step 8: Notification to Applicant
The applicant is notified regarding the approval of the application and issuance of the Digitally Signed Sanction Order.
Step 9: Forwarding to Senior Account Officer
The Anudan Section forwards the approved Sanction Order electronically to the Senior Account Officer through the system for financial processing and record maintenance.
Step 10: Application Closure
After successful issuance of the Sanction Order and completion of all processing formalities, the application status is updated as Completed and the case is formally closed in the system.
