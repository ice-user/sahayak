Workshop in Classical Dance
Classical DanceIndian MusicKala AcademyWorkshop
Details
The Workshop in Classical Dance scheme, implemented by the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa, aims to provide students with classical dance training under the guidance of experts. On the concluding day of the workshop, participants showcase their skills through a dance performance.
Appointment of conductor : The Advisory Committee of Indian Music & Dance recommends to appoint experts to conduct the workshop.
 

Benefits
Financial involvement : Token Honorarium will be paid
 

Eligibility
A student interested in learning classical dance.
 

Application Process
Offline
How to apply : Applications are invited by releasing press notes in local dailies.
Authority for receiving suggestions : Member Secretary, Kala Academy
Authority for redressal of grievances : Member Secretary/Chairman, Kala Academy 
 

Documents Required
Identity proof (Aadhaar Card or other valid ID).
Proof of residence in Goa, if required.
Student ID card or school/college certificate, if applicable.
