Details
The scheme "Assistance to OBM Boat Owner Fishermen on Purchase of Kerosene/Petrol" has been launched by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to fishermen owning small boats with Outboard Motors (OBM) by subsidizing the cost of kerosene or petrol. This initiative supports the fishing community and promotes fisheries in Gujarat.
 

Benefits
Assistance under the scheme:
Name of Component Standard of Assistance Minimum Time Limit (in Years) for Reclaiming Benefits
Assistance on the purchase of Kerosene/Petrol Maximum assistance of ₹50/- per liter Benefits are available as per the prescribed quota throughout the year.
 

Eligibility
The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
The applicant must be a fisherman.
The applicant must be a Kerosene Card holder.
The applicant must own a small boat operated by an Outboard Motor (OBM).
 

Application Process
Offline
Step 1: Fishermen must purchase kerosene or petrol from government-approved fuel stations Gujarat Fisheries Central Co-operative Association Limited (G.F.C.C.A. Ltd.).
Step 2: Collect original bills of purchase.
Step 3: Submit an application along with the required documents to the concerned district office.
Documents Required
Kerosene/Petrol Card issued by the government
Original purchase bills of kerosene/petrol
Boat ownership documents
Aadhaar Card or any government-issued ID proof
Bank account details
Any other document if required
