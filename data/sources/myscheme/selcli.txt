The Lokpal of India
Scheme for Engagement of Law Clerks in Lokpal of India
Law ClerksLegal ResearchOpportunityPublic AdministrationRemuneration
Details
The Lokpal of India has introduced the “Scheme for Engagement of Law Clerks in Lokpal of India” to support its judicial and administrative functions. The scheme provides an opportunity for young law graduates to gain exposure to the functioning of anti-corruption institutions and develop professional skills in legal research, drafting, and public administration. Law clerks assist the Chairperson and Members in research, case file management, drafting, and policy-related work.
 

Benefits
Consolidated remuneration of ₹80,000 per month
Increased remuneration of ₹90,000 per month for extended term (if approved)
Opportunity to work with the Chairperson and Members of Lokpal
Exposure to legal research, drafting, and policy-making
Practical experience in handling real case matters and institutional processes
 

Eligibility
The applicant must be a Law Graduate with a Bachelor’s Degree in Law from an institution recognized by the Bar Council of India
The applicant's age should be between 20 and 32 years as on the last date of application
The applicant must not be engaged in any other employment or assignment. 
The applicant should not have been involved in any criminal case, whether pending or convicted, for an offence involving moral turpitude.
The applicant must not be facing any disciplinary proceeding before the Bar Council of India, Bar Council of the State or Institutes or any other authority.
Candidates in their final year (5-year integrated or 3-year law course) are also eligible (subject to proof before joining)
The applicant must possess:
Research and analytical skills
Good writing ability
Knowledge of computers and legal research tools (e.g., eSCR, SCC Online, Manupatra, LexisNexis, Westlaw)
 

 

Application Process
Offline
Step 1: The interested applicant downloads the application form (Annexure 1) from the official website. 
Step 2: In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
Step 3: Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents as per the instructions mentioned in the official advertisement.
Step 4: Eligible candidates will be called for a written test and/or an interview (viva voce).
Step 5: A selection committee constituted by the chairperson will prepare a panel of selected candidates and a waitlist.
 

Documents Required
Proof of age (Birth certificate / Class 10 certificate)
Law degree certificate or proof of being in final year
Academic mark sheets
Identity proof
Detailed resume/CV
Certificates of internships or experience (if any)
Any other documents as specified in the official notification
