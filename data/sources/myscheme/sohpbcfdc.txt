Schemes of HP Backward Classes Finance & Development Corporation
Backward ClassesBelow Poverty LineDevelopment CorporationFinanceLoan
Details
The Scheme "Backward Classes Finance & Development Corporation", has been launched by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Himachal Pradesh, aiming to uplift the social, educational, and economic status of people belonging to notified backward classes living below the poverty line. Under the Swarnima Yojana, loans are provided to women living below the poverty line from backward classes for self-employment at the rate of 5% interest.
 

Benefits
Loans from ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000/- are provided at 6% interest for activities such as agriculture, handicrafts, traditional business, small and cottage industries, and transport services.
Loans above ₹5,00,000/- are provided at an interest rate of 7%.
Under the Swarnima Yojana, women from backward classes living below the poverty line are given self-employment loans at 5% interest.
Interest-free education loans up to ₹75,000/- are provided for higher education to students whose family income is below ₹98,000/- per year.
For technical and vocational education, loans up to ₹5,00,000/- are available at 4% interest for boys and 3.5% interest for girls.
 

 

Eligibility
The applicant should be a resident of Himachal Pradesh.
The applicant should belong to a backward class.
The applicant’s age should be between 18 and 55 years.
Applicants living in rural areas must have an annual income of less than ₹98,000/-.
Applicants living in urban areas must have an annual income of less than ₹1,20,000/-.
 

Application Process
Offline
Step 1: Fill the prescribed application form and attach all required documents.
Step 2: Submit the application to the concerned District Welfare Officer or Tehsil Welfare Officer.
Step 3: After verification, the application is processed by the Corporation.
