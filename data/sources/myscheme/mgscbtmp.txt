Museum Grant Scheme: Capacity Building and Training of Museum Professionals
Financial AssistanceInternational Training ProgrammesInternship Of Museum ProfessionalsNational Training ProgrammesTraining Of Museum Professionals
Details
The scheme "Museum Grant Scheme: Capacity Building and Training of Museum Professionals" by the Ministry of Culture, Government of India, aims to create expertise and specializations in various fields of museum work, such as collection management, design, education and marketing, which are found lacking in most Indian museums. Through this component, financial assistance for Category I museums and Category II museums is provided to institutions to depute in-service museum professionals for training programmes/internships/fellowships/workshops with national and internationally well-known museums, and to meet fund requirements for training under Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) signed by the Ministry of Culture with foreign institutions/museums of repute. The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Culture, Government of India, and the grant-in-aid released is monitored by a two-member committee comprising officers of the Ministry, including a representative from the National Museum Institute.
 

Benefits
Category I: maximum financial assistance of ₹30,00,000/-.
Category II: maximum financial assistance of ₹25,00,000/-.
Maximum assistance under sub-component (i) is 80% of the total estimated cost of the training programme, subject to the ceilings above; the balance cost to be managed by the museum with an undertaking submitted for the same.
Grant covers, under sub-component (i):
Training fee to the host institution.
Foreign and domestic travel for purposes of training.
Subsistence costs (lodging and boarding) for the duration of training.
Purchase of books or other intellectual material necessary for training.
Costs for purchase of stationery and teaching aids.
Travel insurance for foreign travel (wherever mandated by foreign countries).
Costs for visa fees and similar permits.
For sub-component (ii), financial assistance for training of museum professionals selected under a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) signed by the Ministry of Culture with reputed international museums is provided as per the terms and conditions of the MoU signed with the international museum.
Financial grant for the purpose of capacity building and training of museum professionals will be given to a museum once in 3 years.
Mode of Disbursement:
For sub-component (i): released by the Ministry of Culture in 2 instalments.
Release of the 2nd instalment is subject to receipt of a Utilization Certificate as per GFR 2017 with its Annexures, interest earned on the Ministry's grant, and a financial year-wise statement of accounts audited by a firm of Chartered Accountants, in respect of the previous instalment as well as the museum's matching share; and submission of a full report by the candidate on their training.
For sub-component (ii): fund release is governed by the terms and conditions of the MoU signed with international museums.
Frequency of Disbursement:
1st instalment (75% of the Ministry's share): sanctioned and released after obtaining approval of the Competent Authority.
2nd instalment (remaining 25%): released after the grantee has utilized 100% of the 1st instalment along with its own contribution (20% in case of Central/State Government Museums; 30% in case of other museums).
Validity:
For sub-component (i), the duration of any capacity building or training programme may range from a minimum of 2 weeks to a maximum of 2 years.
For sub-component (ii), duration is governed by the terms and conditions of the MoU signed with international museums.
 

Eligibility
Applicable to sub-component (i) of the Scope:
Category I:
The applicant must be a museum under the Central and State Governments.
Category II:
The applicant must be a museum registered as a Society, voluntary institution, or Trust under the Indian Societies Act of 1860 or similar legislation.
The applicant institution should have been in existence after registration for at least 3 years prior to application.
The applicant should have a well-defined constitution and laid-down rules/bye-laws for its functioning.
The applicant should be in ownership and possession of a substantial collection of objects of historical/cultural/scientific importance that are on public display at all times; the nature and number of objects should be clearly indicated in the proposal.
The applicant museum should be for public service and not-for-profit.
Eligibility of the nominated candidate:
The candidate should be an Indian national.
The candidate should have a basic qualification of Graduation in any subject.
The candidate proposed to be sent on training should have at least 3 years' experience working in/for a museum, and should produce relevant supporting documents even where they have not worked with the applicant institution for 3 years.
Preference/Conditions:
For sub-component (i), the proposed international institution/museum must have agreed in writing to host the museum candidate, or provided an invitation letter expressing interest in hosting the participant, prior to making the application.
Sanction and release of grant is subject to submission of the original confirmation letter of admission from the foreign institution/museum, and a valid passport (not expiring within 6 months of the application) for undertaking foreign travel, if necessary.
The 3-year institutional-existence condition for Category II applicants can be relaxed in exceptional and deserving cases at the discretion of the Secretary (Culture), with reasons recorded in writing.
In the case of candidates from non-government museums registered as trusts/societies, the museum must: display the Ministry of Culture logo and name prominently at the museum's entry for 3 years from the date of grant release; acknowledge the Ministry's support in the final training report; and obtain a letter from the international institution/museum acknowledging the Ministry of Culture's contribution.
 

Exclusions
Individuals who do not work with a museum/institution and only undertake freelance work will not be supported through this grant.
Assistance will not be provided for participation in international conferences or seminars under this scheme.
 

Application Process
Online
Step 1: The applicant visits www.csms.nic.in and applies online; the scheme is open throughout the year with no fixed last date for submission of proposals.
Step 2: The applicant institution sends a covering letter, including nomination of its candidate(s) for the capacity building and training programme, in the prescribed Form C along with the annexures/documents listed in the document section, describing the institution's and candidate's suitability for the training.
Step 3: Applications are processed and appraised on a first-come-first-served basis.
Step 4: Applications received for financial assistance are scrutinized by an Expert Committee consisting of the Joint Secretary (Museums) as Chairman, and Director/Deputy Secretary (Museums) and Director/Deputy Secretary (Finance) as Members.
Step 5: Once the Expert Committee's recommendation is accepted by the Competent Authority (Secretary, Culture), the concerned Joint Secretary releases funds from time to time, in instalments, in consultation with the Integrated Finance Division of the Ministry.
Step 6: A two-member committee comprising officers from the Ministry of Culture, including a representative from the National Museum Institute, monitors the grant-in-aid released to the institutions.
 

Documents Required
 Application Form C (covering letter including nomination of candidate(s), with the Details of the Museum/Applicant Institution)
Registration Certificate of the museum/institution
Copy of the Memorandum of Association of the Institution
Copy of the Rules/Bye-laws of the Society/Trust
Copies of the Audited Statement of Accounts for the last 3 years
Annual Report/Activity Report for the last 3 years
Details of the Proposed Capacity Building/Training Programme with complete budget estimates (Annexure-X)
Details of the Candidate Being Nominated for Training (Annexure-XI) — separate forms for each candidate
Statement signed by the Head of the Institution on how the museum will benefit from training the candidate under the proposed training programme
Details of Other Sources of Funding the project
Letter of Intent/Invitation or Acceptance Letter from the international institution willing to host the candidate for training
Certificate as per Rule 230(1) of GFR 2017 in the prescribed proforma regarding non-receipt of grant for the same purpose from any other Government of India institution (Annexure-IV)
Authorisation Letter for Sending Grant Directly into Bank (Annexure-V)
Original Confirmation Letter of Admission from the foreign institution/museum, and a valid passport not expiring within 6 months of the application (where foreign travel is necessary for training)
