Himachal Pradesh
Hops Cultivation Services
AgriculturalFarmersFarmingSubsidy
Details
Under the Hops Cultivation Services scheme by the Horticulture Department, Government of Himachal Pradesh, the objective is to promote and develop hops cultivation in the state by providing advanced training, quality planting material and other essential agricultural inputs to farmers.
 

Benefits
Individual farmers receive a 50% subsidy on hops production inputs, subject to a maximum limit of ₹10,000.
Hops Societies/Panchayats receive a 50% subsidy on hops production inputs, subject to a maximum limit of ₹50,000.
Hops Societies/Panchayats receive a 50% subsidy on hops processing inputs, subject to a maximum limit of ₹50,000.
A support price for the procurement of dried hops is fixed by the Government from time to time.
 

 

Eligibility
The applicant should be a farmer.
 

Application Process
Offline
The eligible person can apply on the prescribed form and submit it in the concerned Department.
 

Documents Required
Aadhaar Card.
Identity Proof.
Address Proof / Himachali Bonafide Certificate.
Land Ownership Record
Bank Account Details.
Passport-size Photograph.
