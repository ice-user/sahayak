Details
The scheme "Emeritus Fellowship in Biotechnology (EFB)" has been launched by the Science & Technology Department, Government of Odisha. It aims to utilize the knowledge and experience of superannuated teachers and scientists for the advantage of students of Biotechnology at the Post-Graduate level in different Universities of the state. Through this scheme, a consolidated honorarium of ₹20,000/- per month is provided to eligible superannuated teachers and scientists. The scheme is implemented by the Biotechnology Cell of the Science & Technology Department, Government of Odisha. The Fellowship is awarded for 2 years, with 2 fellowships every year. Applications are placed before an Expert Committee for recommendation and Government approval. The Fellowship grant is credited monthly to the awardee’s bank account by the Head of the Institution.
Objectives
To utilize the knowledge and experience of superannuated teachers and scientists for the advantage of students of Biotechnology of different Universities of the state at Post-Graduate level.
 

Benefits
A consolidated honorarium of ₹20,000/- per month is provided to each selected Emeritus Fellow in Biotechnology. 
The Fellowship is awarded for only 2 years. 
A total of 2 fellowships will be awarded every year. 
Mode of Disbursement
The amount of the Fellowship grant shall be directly credited to the bank account of the awardees every month by the Head of the Institution.
 

Eligibility
The applicant must have superannuated, for not more than 1 year, from the post of Professor/Scientist or equivalent. 
The applicant can also apply if he/she is likely to be superannuated within next 6 months. 
The applicant may be an incumbent University Grants Commission (UGC)/Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) Emeritus Fellow. 
The applicant must have at least a Ph.D. degree and 10 years of teaching/research experience in any field of Biotechnology/Bioscience. 
The applicant must have quality research evidenced from published works in Journals. 
The applicant should not hold any other post or be gainfully employed anywhere at the time of joining the Emeritus Fellowship award.
 

Exclusions
The Fellowship shall be cancelled if it is found that the candidate has furnished incorrect information or suppressed materials. In such cases, other disciplinary action will also be initiated against the candidate as deemed fit to recover the fellowship amount. 
The Fellowship shall be cancelled if the conduct of the Fellow is unsatisfactory.
 

Application Process
Offline
Step 1: The Science & Technology Department, Government of Odisha, invites applications from eligible candidates through advertisement in local newspapers and/or the website of the Department. 
Step 2: The application format and the guidelines are made available on the website www.orissa.gov.in/sciencetechnology. The Application Form for Emeritus Fellowship in Biotechnology is also provided in the Guidelines document (Pages 5–6). 
Step 3: The applicant fills out the Application Form for Emeritus Fellowship in Biotechnology and submits the application through the proper channel, including Academic Credential with supporting evidence and Research Achievements with supporting evidence. 
Step 4: Applications received from eligible candidates through the proper channel are placed before an Expert Committee constituted by the Science & Technology Department, Government of Odisha. 
Step 5: The applicants are invited to give a brief presentation on their teaching and research activities and achievements before the Expert Committee. 
Step 6: Government approval is sought for the award of Fellowship based on the recommendation of the Expert Committee. 
Step 7: If selected, the Fellow has to join within 1 month of issue of the appointment letter and submit the Joining Report certifying joining under the Scheme of Science & Technology, Government of Odisha. 
Notes
The Application Form for Emeritus Fellowship in Biotechnology is available in the Guidelines document (Pages 5–6). 
The Department/University/Institute will provide all facilities required for teaching if the applicant is selected as an Emeritus Fellow in Biotechnology. 
The Joining Report must certify that the awardee is not a recipient of emoluments from any other source after joining the Emeritus Fellowship for the period it is sanctioned.
 

Documents Required

Academic Credential With Supporting Evidence 
Research Achievements With Supporting Evidence 
Joining Report (After Selection)
