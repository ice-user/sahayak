Odisha
Innovative Agro Forestry for Food & Nutrition Security: Revolving Fund to Women Self Help Groups
AgroforestryFund AssistanceSeedlingsWSHG
Details
The scheme “Innovative Agroforestry for Food & Nutrition Security” has been launched by the Agriculture & Farmers’ Empowerment Department, Government of Odisha. It aims to promote agroforestry through institutional support mechanisms. Through this scheme, revolving fund assistance is provided to Women Self-Help Groups for raising agroforestry, vegetable, and nutrition garden seedlings. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Soil Conservation & Watersheds through district and block-level institutions. The intervention supports the establishment of nurseries and seedling production as an enterprise.
Objectives
To ensure the availability of quality planting material.
To strengthen Women Self-Help Groups through nursery-based livelihood activities.
To popularise various Agroforestry practices/models suitable to different agro-ecological regions and land use conditions.
To provide extension and capacity building support to the agroforestry sector.
 

Benefits
Financial assistance is provided for Nursery Infrastructure Development at ₹10,00,000/- per unit.
Revolving Fund for raising agroforestry seedlings is provided at ₹3,00,000/- per unit.
Revolving Fund to Women Self Help Groups for raising Pro Tray seedlings for Nutrition Garden is provided at ₹25,000/- per unit.
Assistance for Capacity Building is provided up to 5% of the Annual Budget.
Assistance for Monitoring and Evaluation is provided up to 2% of the Annual Budget.
Assistance for Administrative Expenses is provided up to 2% of the Annual Budget.
 

Eligibility
The applicant group must reside in the state of Odisha.
The applicant group must be a Women's Self-Help Group with a minimum of 1 year of experience.
The Women's Self-Help Group must be willing to take up vegetable nursery raising as an enterprise.
The Women Self-Help Group must be involved in collective on-farm activities, preferably in a collective mode.
Prior experience of nursery raising/ exposure to subject-specific trainings/ availability of basic infrastructure (shed, net/nursery tools/water lifting devices, etc. will be an added advantage.
The Women Self Help Group must possess 500 sq. ft. of land suitable for raising vegetable seedlings and a water source nearby.
The applicant group must also have prior experience in raising vegetable seedlings.
 

Application Process
Offline
Step 1: The Women Self-Help Group submits an application for availing revolving fund assistance in the prescribed application format appended to the guidelines (Annexure III / relevant application annexure as applicable for institutional beneficiaries). 
Step 2: In the application, the Women Self Help Group furnishes details of the group, bank account particulars, resolution of the group, and details of the proposed intervention, including nursery infrastructure development, raising of agroforestry seedlings, or pro-tray seedlings for nutrition gardens, as provided in the application annexure.
Step 3: The application also includes details of the proposed nursery site, availability of land and water source, and proposed seedling production activities, as required under the scheme norms and annexure formats.
Step 4: The submitted application is received by the concerned field functionary at the block or district level for preliminary scrutiny.
Step 5: Field officials conduct verification of the eligibility of the Women Self Help Group, feasibility of the proposed nursery activity, and suitability of the site through physical inspection, as per the verification and monitoring provisions of the guidelines.
Step 6: After verification, the proposal is examined at the block and district levels and consolidated as part of the district Annual Action Plan, following the implementation framework laid down in the guidelines.
Step 7: The consolidated proposals are placed before the competent approving authority for approval, in accordance with the scheme implementation procedure.
Step 8: Upon approval, the revolving fund assistance is released to the Women Self Help Group as per the admissible cost norms and pattern of assistance specified in Annexure IV (Cost Norms of Different Interventions).
Step 9: The Women Self-Help Group utilises the revolving fund for the approved nursery and seedling raising activities and maintains necessary records as prescribed.
Step 10: Monitoring, reporting, and evaluation of utilisation of funds and progress of nursery activities are carried out by designated officials, as per the Monitoring and Evaluation provisions of the guidelines.
