Rajasthan
Strengthening of Existing Canal System
Canal StrengtheningIrrigation InfrastructureTribal Area IrrigationWater Resource Management
Details
The scheme “Strengthening of Existing Canal System” by the Tribal Area Development Department, Government of Rajasthan, aims to strengthen and expand canal irrigation systems by providing financial support for restoration and extension of canals to benefit farmers, especially tribal cultivators, in tribal dominated areas.
 

Benefits
Canal Strengthening:
Repair and strengthening of existing irrigation canals constructed under major, medium, and minor irrigation schemes.
Restoration of damaged canal systems in tribal areas.
Canal Expansion:
Extension of canal networks to increase irrigation coverage for agricultural lands in tribal regions.
Improvement in Irrigation Facilities:
Increased irrigation capacity leads to improved agricultural productivity for farmers.
 

Eligibility
The applicant should be a farmer.
The applicant should belong mainly to the Scheduled Tribe community.
The project area should be such that more than 50% of the irrigated land belongs to Scheduled Tribe farmers.
The number of beneficiary tribal farmers should exceed 50% of the total farmers in the project area.
 

Application Process
Offline
Step 1: A proposal is prepared for canal strengthening or expansion as per project requirements.
Step 2: The proposal is submitted to the Project Officer, Tribal Area Development Department (TAD).
 

Documents Required
Land details of beneficiary farmers.
