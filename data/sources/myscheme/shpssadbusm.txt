Uttarakhand
Vishesh Samman Pension Yojana for the State Agitators who became Disabled and Completely Bedridden during the Uttarakhand State Movement
BedriddenDisabilityDisabled PersonPensionUttarakhand State Movement
Details
The "Vishesh Samman Pension Yojana for the State Agitators who became Disabled and Completely Bedridden during the Uttarakhand State Movement" scheme is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to honor state agitators who became permanently disabled and bedridden during the Uttarakhand Statehood Movement by providing them with monthly financial assistance.
 

Benefits
Pension Amount: ₹20,000/- per month
 

Eligibility
The applicant must be a participant of the Uttarakhand Statehood Movement.
The applicant must have become permanently disabled and completely bedridden due to incidents during the Uttarakhand Statehood Movement.
 

Application Process
Offline
The application must be submitted to the concerned District Magistrate. Based on the report or recommendation of the District Magistrate, the pension will be sanctioned at the Government level after obtaining approval from the Hon’ble Departmental Minister or the Hon’ble Chief Minister.
Although a prescribed application format is not available, applicants seeking the Special Honor Pension must submit their application to the office of the concerned District Magistrate, along with a certificate issued by the State Medical Board or a Medical College confirming that the applicant is completely bedridden.
 

Documents Required
Identity proof
A certificate issued by the State Medical Board or a Medical College confirming that the applicant is completely bedridden
Other documents as required
