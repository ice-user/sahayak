National Family Benefit Scheme -Meghalaya
Below Poverty LineDeathFamilyPension
Details
The Community and Rural Development Department, Government of Meghalaya, implements the National Family Benefit Scheme. This scheme aims to provide one-time financial assistance to Below Poverty Line (BPL) families who have lost their primary breadwinner, helping them cope with the financial difficulties caused by the loss.
 

 

Benefits
Eligible families receive one-time financial assistance of ₹20,000 following the death of the primary breadwinner.
 

Eligibility
The applicant should be a resident of Meghalaya.
The family must belong to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.
The deceased person must have been the primary breadwinner of the family.
The deceased primary breadwinner must have been between 18 and 59 years of age at the time of death.
 

Application Process
Offline
Interested applicants should visit the Village Authorities and the Office of the Block Development Officer.
 

Documents Required
Death Certificate.
Proof of Identity.
Proof of Address.
BPL Card / Ration Card of the Family.
Family ID / Member ID.
