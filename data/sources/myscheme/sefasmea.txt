Scheme for Extending Financial Assistance to Sugar Mills for Enhancement and Augmentation of Ethanol Production Capacity
DistilleriesDual Feed DistilleriesEthanol ProductionGrain Based DistilleriesInterest SubventionSugar MillsSugarcane
Details
The scheme “Scheme for Extending Financial Assistance to Sugar Mills for Enhancement and Augmentation of Ethanol Production Capacity” implemented by the Department of Food and Public Distribution (DFPD), Government of India, aims to increase ethanol production and supply under the Ethanol Blended with Petrol Programme through providing interest subvention on loans to sugar mills, distilleries, and entrepreneurs. The scheme supports setting up new distilleries, expansion of existing distilleries, conversion to dual feed distilleries, and installation of Molecular Sieve Dehydration (MSDH) columns. Assistance is provided for the production of 1st Generation (1G) ethanol from feedstocks such as cereals, molasses, sugarcane, sugar beet, sorghum, and other approved feedstocks. The scheme also promotes Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) systems approved by the Central Pollution Control Board. The scheme helps improve liquidity of sugar mills and enables timely clearance of cane price arrears to farmers.
 

Benefits
Interest Subvention: Interest subvention of 6% per annum or 50% of the interest rate charged by the lending institution, whichever is lower.
Loan Coverage: Assistance is provided on the sanctioned and disbursed loan amount based on the approved project capacity.
Duration of Assistance: Interest subvention is available for 5 years, including 1 year moratorium period.
Support for New Distilleries: Financial assistance is available for setting up new grain-based, molasses-based, and dual-feed distilleries.
Support for Expansion: Assistance is available for the expansion of existing ethanol production capacities.
Support for Conversion: Assistance is available for the conversion of existing molasses-based or grain-based distilleries into dual-feed distilleries.
Support for MSDH Installation: Assistance is available for the installation of Molecular Sieve Dehydration (MSDH) columns for ethanol production.
Support for Zero Liquid Discharge: Assistance is available for the installation of approved Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) systems.
 

Eligibility
Assistance under the scheme shall be available to the entrepreneurs for:
Setting up grain-based distilleries/expansion of existing grain-based distilleries to produce ethanol. However, the benefits of the interest subvention scheme is to be extended to only those distilleries which are using or will be using dry milling technique to produce Dry Distillers Grain Soluble (DDGS).
Setting up new molasses-based distilleries/expansion of existing distilleries (whether attached to sugar mills or standalone distilleries) to produce ethanol, and for installing any method approved by Central Pollution Control Board for achieving Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD).
To set up new dual feed distilleries or to expand existing capacities of dual feed distilleries.
To convert existing molasses based distilleries (whether attached to sugar mills or standalone distilleries) to dual feed (molasses and grain/ or any other feed stock producing 1G Ethanol); and also to convert grain based distilleries to dual feed.
To set up new distilleries/expansion of existing distilleries to produce ethanol from other feed stocks producing 1G ethanol such as sugar beet, sorghum, cereals, etc.
To install the Molecular Sieve Dehydration (MSDH) column to convert rectified spirit to ethanol in the existing distilleries.
 

Application Process
Online
Step 1: The applicant must prepare the application cum proposal in the prescribed format (Annexure-I).
Step 2: The applicant must submit the application online through the DFPD portal.
Step 3: The applicant must submit the application within the notified application window announced by DFPD.
Step 4: The Screening Committee and Approval Committee scrutinize the applications.
Step 5: Eligible applicants receive in-principle approval from DFPD.
Step 6: The approved proposal is forwarded to banks or financial institutions for loan sanction.
Step 7: The applicant must obtain loan disbursement within 1 year from the date of approval.
Step 8: The applicant must complete the project within the prescribed timeline and submit the required certificates.
