Kushal Yuva Program-Bihar
Communication SkillsComputer KnowledgeEmploymentSkill DevelopmentYouth
Details
The scheme "Kushal Yuva Program" by the Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar aims to improve employability through providing 240 hours of language, basic computer, and behavioral skills training to unemployed youth aged 15 to 25 years. It seeks to fulfill the skill development objective under the 7 Resolves of the Government of Bihar by making youth aware of various employment opportunities.
 

Benefits
Language and Communication Skills Training: Provides 80 hours of training in Hindi and English language and communication skills to improve workplace interaction.
Basic Computer Knowledge Training: Provides 120 hours of foundational computer training to build digital literacy.
Behavioral Skills Training: Provides 40 hours of soft skills and behavioral training to prepare youth for professional environments.
Total Training Duration: Delivers a combined total of 240 hours of structured training completed over a period of 3 months.
Training Material and Infrastructure: Provides free training materials from the Bihar Skill Development Mission along with proper seating, drinking water, and toilet facilities at Block Skill Development Centres and private centres.
Refundable Security Deposit: Applicants not receiving the Self Help Allowance must deposit ₹1,000/- as a security deposit to the Bihar Skill Development Mission through the centre, which is refunded upon successful completion of the training.
Release of Self Help Allowance: For applicants receiving the Self Help Allowance, the final 5 months of allowance is released only after submitting the certificate of successful completion of this training.
Certification: Provides formal certification upon completing 100 percent of the training on time and passing the final examination.
 

Eligibility
The applicant must be a permanent resident of the state of Bihar.
The applicant must be an unemployed youth.
The applicant must be between 15 and 25 years of age.
The applicant must not be currently studying.
The applicant must be actively seeking employment.
The applicant must have passed at least Class 10 or Class 12 from a government-recognized institution affiliated with a recognized board or examination council located in the state.
The applicant aged 20 to 25 years who receives the Self Help Allowance must mandatorily undergo this training.
The applicant aged 15 to 25 years who has passed Class 10 and is unable to pursue further education may apply solely for this training.
The applicant must maintain discipline, attend classes on time, complete 100 percent of the training, and pass the final examination.
 

Application Process
Online
Step 1: Visit the offcial website, click "New Registration," enter your details and captcha, verify the OTP, and receive your username and password via email/SMS.
Step 2: Log in on the homepage using the received credentials, change your password, and log in again with your new password.
Step 3: Fill in the Personal Information Page, click "Submit," confirm by clicking "OK," and click "Next."
Step 4: Select "Kushal Yuva Program" from the drop-down menu, click "Apply," complete the form, and sign the declaration.
Step 5: Download or print the PDF acknowledgement (also sent via email) and note the registration ID sent via SMS.
Step 6: Wait for the DRCC Manager to schedule your appointment and notify you of the visit date via email and SMS.
Step 7: Visit the DRCC on the allocated date with self-attested document copies (Aadhaar, 10th/12th certificates, Resident Certificate, and Common Application Form), collect a token, complete verification and photo capture with the MPA, and receive an acknowledgement slip.
