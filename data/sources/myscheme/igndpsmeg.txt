Meghalaya
Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme-Meghalaya
Below Poverty LineDisabilityFinancial SupportPension
Details
The Community and Rural Development Department, Government of Meghalaya, implements the Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme. This scheme aims to provide financial assistance to persons with severe or multiple disabilities belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) families and support their basic living needs.
 

Benefits
Eligible beneficiaries receive financial assistance of up to ₹500 per month.
 

Eligibility
The applicant must be between 18 and 79 years of age.
The applicant must belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.
The applicant must have a severe or multiple disability as specified under the scheme guidelines.
 

Application Process
Offline
Interested applicants should visit the Village Authorities and the Office of the Block Development Officer.
 

Documents Required
Identity Proof (Voter ID Card/ Driving License)
Disability Certificate
Proof of Age
Address Proof
Bank Account Details
