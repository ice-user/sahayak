Rajasthan
Balika Protsahan Yojana (For Class 12th)
Class 12th ScholarshipGirl EducationIncentiveMeritorious Students
Details
The scheme “Girl Child Incentive (For Class 12th)” launched by the Education (Elementary and Secondary) Department, Government of Rajasthan aims to promote higher education among meritorious girl students by providing financial incentives to those who secure 75% or more marks in Higher Secondary Arts, Commerce, Science and Senior Upadhyaya Examination conducted by Board of Secondary Education
 

Benefits
Financial Incentive for Meritorious Girls: Eligible girl students receive incentives as per the norms.
 

Eligibility
The applicant must be a native of Rajasthan.
The applicant must be a girl student.
The applicant must have secured 75% or more marks in Higher Secondary Arts, Commerce, Science, or Senior Secondary Examinations conducted by the Board of Secondary Education, Ajmer.
 

Application Process
Online
The applicant must visit the official website of the Education Department, Rajasthan: https://rajshaladarpan.nic.in/SD1/Home/BSF/Index.aspx 
 

Documents Required
1. Domicile Certificate.
2. School Certificate.
3. Bank Account (in the child's name)
4. Class 12th Marksheet.
5. Passport Size Photograph.
6. Mobile Number.
