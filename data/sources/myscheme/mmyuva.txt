Uttar Pradesh
Mukhya Mantri Yuva Udyami Vikas Abhiyan (CM-YUVA)
Interest SubventionLoanSelf-EmploymentSkilled Youth
Details
The "Mukhya Mantri Yuva Udyami Vikas Abhiyan" scheme launched by the MSME and Export Promotion Department, Uttar Pradesh. The scheme aims to connect skilled young people with self-employment by establishing production/service-related enterprises through providing interest-free and guarantee-free loans, interest subvention, and margin money.
 

Benefits
First Phase
100% interest free collateral guarantee free loan for 04 years for projects up to ₹5,00,000/-.
General category beneficiaries will have to contribute 15% of the project cost, OBC 12.5%, SC/ST/Divyang 10% and beneficiaries of aspirational districts- Chitrakoot, Chandauli, Fatehpur, Balrampur, Siddharthnagar, Shravasti and Bahraich will have to contribute 10% of their own contribution.
10% margin money grant on project cost and CGTMSE reimbursement for 04 years.
Land and building will not be included in the project cost and it will be mandatory to have a minimum of 10% of the total project cost in the form of term loan.
Second Phase
The candidate who makes full refund of the principal/penal interest taken in the first stage will be eligible.
Projects can be set up up to ₹10,00,000/-, but 50% interest subsidy will be given on loans up to ₹7,50,000/- for 03 years.
The loan must have a minimum term loan of 10% of the project cost and may include the cost of workshop/workshed.
CGTMSE coverage for 03 years.
 

Eligibility
The applicant age should be between 21 to 40 years.
The applicant educational qualification should be minimum class 8th passed, Intermediate or equivalent preferred.
The applicant should have trained under Vishwakarma Shram Samman Yojana, ODOP Training Scheme, SC/ST/OBC Training Scheme, UP Skill Development Mission or should have obtained skill related certificate/degree/diploma etc. from any recognized educational institution.
 

Application Process
Online
Step 1: First, the applicant will start their registration by visiting the portal: https://msme.cmyuva.org.in/home. Click on "Login" > "Applicant Login" > "New User Registration".
Step 2: After this, a new pop-up page will open on the portal where the applicant will fill in all the fields, which include the Name of the Scheme (for which the application is being made; Select "Mukhyamantri Yuva Udyami Vikas Abhiyaan"), Applicant's Name, Date of Birth, Father's Name, Mobile Number, Email, District Name, and Security Code (Captcha).
Step 3: If the applicant has already registered, they will directly come to the Registered User Login and click the Login button after entering the User ID, Password, and Captcha. 
Step 4: The applicant log in again with their User ID and new password by going to the portal's login page.
Step 5: The applicant can obtain the list of all mandatory attachments required for the schemes available on the portal by clicking the relevant scheme under "Important Instructions for Online Beneficiary-Oriented Scheme".
Step 6: After this, the applicant will fill the E-Form (Online Application) in the following three steps:
 The applicant will enter all the fields available in the E-Form (Online Application).
Upload all the attachments (documents) as per the scheme.
 Take a printout of the Affidavit (if required in the scheme) and upload the copy verified by a Notary.
Step 7: The applicant should take a printout of the draft copy of the application and properly check all the entries. If there is any error in the application, they should correct it by going to the relevant option.
Step 8: After this, they should finalize the submission by going to the "Application Copy" option. Once the application is finally submitted, it cannot be modified.
Step 9: After the final submission of the application, take a printout of the application copy.
Step 10: The applicant can get the updated status of the application by entering the application number on the "Application Status" option available on the portal.
