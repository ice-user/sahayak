Rajasthan
Economic Viability Award for Girls with Physical Disabilities
Financial AssistanceGirlsPhysical DisabilitySpecial Needs
Details
The scheme “Economic Viability Award for Girls with Physical Disabilities” launched by the Education (Elementary and Secondary) Department, Government of Rajasthan, aims to support physically disabled girls studying in Government schools by providing financial assistance to encourage continued education and economic stability.
 

Benefits
Financial Assistance: Provides monetary support to girls with physical disabilities studying in Classes 1 to 12 in Government schools.
 

Eligibility
The applicant must be a girl.
The applicant must be a student.
 The applicant must be studying in a Government school.
The applicant must be enrolled in any class from 1 to 12.
The applicant must be physically disabled.
 

Application Process
Offline
Please contact your school.
 

Documents Required
Disability Certificate .
Domicile Certificate.
School Certificate.
