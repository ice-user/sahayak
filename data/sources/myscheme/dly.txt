Delhi Lakshmi Yojana
Direct Benefit TransferFinancial AssistanceWomen Welfare
Details
The "Delhi Lakshmi Yojana" is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Delhi to provide monthly financial assistance to eligible women. Under the scheme, financial assistance of ₹2,500/- per month is transferred directly to the beneficiary's bank account through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mode. The scheme is intended for eligible women who satisfy the prescribed age, income, residency, and other eligibility conditions. Registration for the scheme starts from 1st August, 2026.
 

Benefits
Monthly Financial Assistance: An amount of ₹2,500/- per month is provided to each eligible beneficiary through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the beneficiary's bank account.
 

Eligibility
Applicant must be Female.
Applicant should be the eldest female member in the family.
The applicant's age must be between 21 to 60 years as on date of submission of application.
Annual family income should not exceed ₹2,50,000/-.
The applicant or her husband or either of her parents, must have been a resident of Delhi for a minimum period of 10 years as on date of submission of application.
Applicant should be registered voter of Delhi.
Annual electricity consumption of the family during the previous 12 months should not exceed 2,400 units.
The applicant should not be receiving financial assistance in the form of any pension provided by the central or state government, or should not be an income tax payee/ GST Filer, should not be a Government Servant and/or holding a public office.
Neither the applicant nor any family member should be employed as a regular or contractual employee in the State Government, Government of India, any Public Sector Undertaking, Board, Local Body, Government Organisation, or is receiving widow pension, disability pension or any other pension, financial assistance or similar.
Neither the applicant nor any family member should own a four-wheeler motor vehicle.
Applicant should not have more than 3 children.
Applicant should not have any criminal antecedents or criminal record.

Application Process
Online
Registration Process:
Step 1: Visit the official website and click on "Login | Register" button.
Step 2: Click on "Don't have an account? Register Now".
Step 3: Enter the details and click on "Continue".
Step 4: Registration Id shall be generated.
Application Submission Process:
Step 1: Visit the official website and click on "Login | Register" button.
Step 2: Enter Registration Id, Password and verify the Captcha. Click on "Sign In".
Step 3: Complete all eligibility questions and verify eligibility conditions.
Step 4: Fill personal details along with Aadhaar, basic information and family member details correctly.
Step 5: Provide applicant bank account details for benefit transfer and upload all required documents in the prescribed format.
Step 6: Preview the complete application carefully and submit the final application.
Step 7: Upload signed copy of MP/MLA Endorsement.
Step 8: Download and save the acknowledgement receipt for future reference.
