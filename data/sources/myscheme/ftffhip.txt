Free Travel Facility for Hearing Impaired Persons
Differently AbledFree Travel FacilityHearing Impaired PersonsPerson With DisabilitiesSpecially Abled
Details
The scheme "Free Travel Facility for Hearing Impaired Persons" by the Rajasthan State Transport Corporation under the Transport Department, Government of Rajasthan, aims to facilitate mobility by providing free travel in ordinary, express, and night bus services to hearing impaired residents of Rajasthan.
 

Benefits
Free Travel Facility: Eligible beneficiaries are provided free travel in Rajasthan State Transport Corporation(RSRTC ) buses.
Coverage of Buses: The facility is applicable on ordinary, express, and night bus services.
Route Applicability: Free travel is allowed up to the routes where Rajasthan State Transport Corporation buses are operational.
 

Eligibility
The applicant should be a resident of Rajasthan.
The applicant should not be an income tax payer.
The applicant should be a hearing-impaired person certified by an authorized subject specialist.
