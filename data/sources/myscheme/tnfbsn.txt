Nagaland
The National Family Benefit Scheme (Nagaland)
Below Poverty LineBreadwinnerDeathFamilyPension
Details
The National Family Benefit Scheme under the National Social Assistance Programme is implemented by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Nagaland. The scheme provides a one-time financial assistance of ₹20,000 to a bereaved household in the event of the death of its primary breadwinner. For the purpose of this scheme, a woman who is a homemaker is also recognized as a breadwinner. The financial assistance is granted to the surviving member who, after a local inquiry, is identified as the head of the household. Under the scheme, the term “household” includes the spouse, minor children, unmarried daughters, and dependent parents of the deceased. In the case of the death of an unmarried adult, the household is considered to include minor brothers and sisters as well as dependent parents.
 

Benefits
Under this scheme, the amount under this scheme is a one-time grant of ₹20,000/-
 

Eligibility
The applicant should be a resident of Nagaland.
The applicant should be a family member or dependent of the deceased breadwinner (an earning member of the family on whom the family primarily depended for income).
The family should be living Below the Poverty Line (BPL).
The deceased should have been between 18 and 60 years old.
 

Application Process
Offline
Step 1:The applicant submits the application at the District Welfare Office.
Step 2:The District Level Committee verifies applications and forwards the selected beneficiaries for approval.
Step 3: After approval, the pension amount is paid in cash by the District Welfare Officer.
 

Documents Required
Death Certificate.
Proof of Identity.
Proof of Address.
BPL Card / Ration Card of the Family.
Family ID / Member ID.
