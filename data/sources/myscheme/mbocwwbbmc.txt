Mizoram Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (MBOCWWB): Benefits for Members' Children
Congratulatory GrantConstruction WorkersEducational AssistanceEducational Assistance
Details
The scheme “Benefits for Members' Children” under the "Mizoram Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board Benefits for Workers" is implemented by the Mizoram Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Government of Mizoram, aims to provide welfare benefits to eligible construction workers and their children. The Board has been constituted under the Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996, and a Welfare Fund has been established. Eligible construction workers between 18 and 60 years of age who have worked for not less than 90 days in a year may apply for membership. Registered members with at least 1 year of continuous registration may receive annual educational assistance for up to 2 children. The educational assistance is transferred directly to the beneficiaries' accounts through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
 

Benefits
Educational Assistance for Members' Children:
KG-I to Class VIII: ₹4,000/- per child annually.
Class IX to Class XII: ₹5,000/- per child annually.
Undergraduate Students: ₹6,000/- per child annually.
Industrial Training Institute/Polytechnic Diploma Course Students: ₹6,000/- per child annually.
Professional & Technical Degree Course Students: ₹20,000/- per child annually.
Research Scholars/Post-Graduate Level Students: ₹20,000/- per child annually.
Number of Children: Educational assistance is available for up to 2 children of a member who has held continuous registration for at least 1 year.
Congratulatory Grant:
Top 10 in Board Examinations: ₹50,000/- each for up to 2 children securing a position within the Top 10 in HSLC, HSSLC, and CBSE examinations.
Distinction: ₹20,000/- each for up to 2 children securing Distinction in HSLC, HSSLC, and CBSE examinations.
 

Eligibility
The applicant must be an Indian citizen.
The applicant must be between 18 and 60 years of age.
The applicant must have been engaged as a construction worker for at least 90 days in a year.
The applicant must be engaged as a contractor or employee in one of the covered categories of construction work.
Contractors or employees engaged in the following categories of work:
1. Carpenter
2. Mason
3. Cement Mason
4. Painter
5. Helper (Construction Works)
6. Electrician
7. Wireman
8. Fitter
9. Welder
10. Plumber
11. Iron and Aluminium Worker
12. Blacksmith
13. Lumberjack
14. Sand Extraction Worker
15. Stone Quarry Worker
16. Stone Breaker
17. Road and Earthwork Labourer
18. JCB/Dozer Operator
19. Construction Machine Operator
20. Construction Driver
21. Brick Factory Worker
Membership Fee:
A one-time Registration Fee of ₹25/- shall be payable at the time of enrolment.
A monthly subscription of ₹10/- is payable to maintain active membership. Alternatively, an advance payment of ₹120/- may be made to cover one year's subscription.
Members wishing to pay the Membership/Renewal Fee in advance may do so for a period of up to three (3) years.
Membership must be renewed within one (1) year from the date of expiry, in accordance with Section 17 of the BOCW (RE&CS) Act, 1996.
 

Application Process
Offline
Step 1: Eligible registered members of the Welfare Board may apply for the respective welfare schemes by submitting the prescribed scheme-specific application form, duly filled in and accompanied by the requisite supporting documents, through the prescribed offline mode. 
Step 2: The application forms and supporting documents shall be as specified by the Welfare Board for the respective scheme/benefit.

Documents Required
Documents Required for Membership:
Photocopy of Voter Identity Card. (Where the Voter I.D. Card does not indicate the applicant's date of birth, a supporting document clearly indicating the year of birth must also be furnished.)
Photocopy of Aadhaar Card (in accordance with Section 142 of the Code on Social Security, 2020).
Attested photocopy of the Family Ration Card.
Attested photocopy of the Bank Passbook.
Two (2) copies of passport-size photographs.
Recommendation/Attestation from the Local Council/Village Council and the concerned Branch YMA/YLA/MTP.
