Meghalaya
Swadhar Greh - A Scheme for women in difficult circumstances
BPL FamiliesGirlViolenceWomen
Details
Swadhar Greh – A Scheme for Women in Difficult Circumstances, under the Social Welfare Department, Government of Meghalaya, aims to provide temporary accommodation, maintenance, and rehabilitative services to women and girls who are rendered homeless due to family discord, crime, violence, mental stress, social ostracism, or who are in moral danger. The scheme also aims to support their economic and emotional rehabilitation.
 

 

Benefits
Eligible women and girls can receive: Training, rehabilitation
Pattern of Assistance: Implementing Agencies may seek assistance for construction and rent.
Strategies: Temporary residential accommodation with provision of basic needs. Vocational Training, Counselling, Legal Aid and Guidance.
 

Eligibility
The applicant should be a woman or girl who is homeless or shelterless.
The applicant should be rendered homeless due to family discord, crime, violence, mental stress, or social ostracism.
The applicant should be in moral danger and require temporary accommodation and rehabilitation support.
The applicant should belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.
 

Application Process
Offline
Applications of suitable organisation in the prescribed format completed with all the required documents are to be submitted through the District Social Welfare Officers concerned.
For details, District Social Welfare Officers of Shillong/Jowai/Nongpoh/Nongstoin/Tura/Shillong/Baghmara may be contacted.
 

Documents Required
Aadhaar Card or any valid identity proof.
Address/Residence proof.
BPL Card, if applicable.
Age proof.
Passport-size photograph.
Any relevant document/proof related to the difficult circumstances, if applicable.
