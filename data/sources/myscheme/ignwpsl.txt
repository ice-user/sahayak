Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (Ladakh)
BPLIndira GandhiPensionWidow
Details
The ‘Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme’ under the National Social Assistance Programme is implemented by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Ladakh. Under this scheme, a monthly pension of ₹1,000 is provided to BPL individuals aged above 49 years, and widows aged 40 to 79 years belonging to the BPL category are also eligible. The scheme is centrally sponsored and jointly funded by the Centre and State Governments.
 

Benefits
Under this scheme, BPL people receive ₹1,000/-p per month.
 

Eligibility
The applicant should be a resident of Ladakh.
The applicant should be a widow.
The widow's age should be above 40 years.
The widow should belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.
 

Application Process
Offline
Step 1: Obtain the application form free of cost from the DSWO/TSWO, fill it completely, and attach proof of age, income, status, and address.
Step 2: Submit the completed application to the concerned Tehsil Social Welfare Officer (TSWO) and get a proper receipt.
Step 3: The TSWO consolidates eligible applications and forwards them to the District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO) for fund arrangement and sanction by the District Level Sanctioning Committee.
 

Documents Required
Aadhaar card of the applicant
Passport-size photograph
Identity proof i.e. Voter ID Card, Driving License
Death certificate of husband
Residence Proof
Proof of age
Below Poverty Line (BPL) Card/related documents
Caste certificate, if applicable
Address proof
Bank Passbook/Bank account details
Any other documents as required.
